About

Aerospace Engineer

I'm an Aerospace Engineering graduate from Purdue University with a minor in AI/ML, working at Relativity Space as a full-time Launch Fluids engineer. I've spent the last two years getting my hands on flight hardware across launch vehicle and spacecraft programs at SpaceX and Rocket Lab, working on various propellant systems, structural components, and mechanical mechanisms all the way from concept through hardware testing and delivery. My past work has spanned guidance, navigation & control, trajectory optimization, spacecraft structures, and launch fluids system design.

I'm drawn to the intersection of rigorous first-principles engineering and modern computational methods, and outside of work am very interested in the intersection of autonomy and aerospace hardware — from drone docking mechanisms to large scale propellent feed systems to interplanetary entry, descent, and landing systems.

Europa Entry, Descent & Landing — MPC & Convex Optimization

Full Entry, Descent & Landing simulation for a Europa lander. Entry controlled via Pontryagin Maximum Principle bang-bang optimal control; descent & landing via Second-Order Cone Programming (SOCP) with receding-horizon MPC and nonlinear RK4 dynamics propagation.

Key Results Solved and simultaed optimal descent profile, with nonlinear dynamics, and quantified the difference from a linearized system with MPC.

ML · NLP · Generative AI

CADformer — Natural Language to CAD

I project I created to take input text and turn it into CAD stl files. This project involves a Five-stage natural language-to-Cad model pipeline combining DECINT-style interleaved decomposition with Gorilla-inspired BM25 retrieval-augmented code generation. Benchmarked on on AI generated input design phrases across three difficulty levels against direct-prompting and Chain-of-thought baselines.

Key Result 86.1% execution success rate vs. 77.8% (DIRECT) and 69.4% (CoT)

Launch Engineering Intern

SpaceX Summer 2024

During my time as a Starship Launch Engineering Intern at SpaceX, I drove high-impact structural and fluid system optimizations for critical launch pad architecture. I owned the redesign of the GCH4 pump supply and venting infrastructure, scaling piping from 4" to 6" to minimize boil-off losses and facilitate complex tank demolition, while utilizing CAESAR II to perform meticulous stress analysis and optimize pipe routing from LCH4 pumps to the heat exchanger skid. Through these projects, I successfully enhanced system fluid efficiency, safeguarded essential launch infrastructure against extreme dynamic plume loads, and streamlined pad operational efficiency.
